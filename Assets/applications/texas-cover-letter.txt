CURRICULUM CONTENT:
--------------------------------------------------------------
Our 6-hour curriculum covers all TEA-required topics with 
Texas-specific content throughout:

Module 1: Introduction & Texas Traffic Laws (55 minutes)
  - Texas Transportation Code overview
  - Texas point system and license suspension thresholds
  - 20-day ticket dismissal request requirement
  - Speed limits (including 85 mph — highest in the U.S.)
  - Texas Hands-Free Law and penalties
  - Move Over/Slow Down Law
  - Right-of-way rules and intersection safety

Module 2: Defensive Driving Techniques (55 minutes)
  - IPDE defensive driving process
    (Identify, Predict, Decide, Execute)
  - Following distance management (3-4 second rule)
  - Speed and stopping distance physics
  - Lane change and passing safety
  - Texas-specific hazards (highway driving, rural roads)

Module 3: Impaired Driving & Texas DWI Laws (55 minutes)
  - Texas DWI laws (not DUI — important distinction)
  - BAC limits: 0.08% (21+), 0.04% (commercial), 
    Zero Tolerance (under 21)
  - Enhanced penalties for BAC 0.15%+
  - Open container law
  - Implied consent and ALR process
  - Drug-impaired driving
  - Consequences: jail, fines, license suspension, surcharges

Module 4: Distracted & Drowsy Driving (55 minutes)
  - Texas Hands-Free Law (2023 effective statewide)
  - Types of distraction (visual, manual, cognitive)
  - School zone and active work zone cell phone bans
  - Drowsy driving risks and prevention
  - In-vehicle technology distractions

Module 5: Sharing Texas Roads (55 minutes)
  - Pedestrian safety and crosswalk laws
  - Bicycle safety (3-foot passing rule)
  - Commercial vehicle awareness (blind spots, stopping distance)
  - Motorcycle awareness
  - School bus laws (stop when lights flash)
  - Emergency vehicle response
  - Railroad crossing safety

Module 6: Texas Weather & Special Conditions (55 minutes)
  - Rain and hydroplaning (first rain on Texas roads)
  - Flooding: "Turn Around, Don't Drown"  
  - Fog driving techniques
  - Ice and winter driving (rare but deadly in Texas)
  - Night driving
  - Construction zone safety (doubled fines)
  - Road rage recognition and avoidance
  - Comprehensive course review and exam preparation

Assessment Structure:
  - 4 quiz questions per module (24 total, 70% passing)
  - 20-question final exam (multiple choice, 80% passing)
  - Unlimited retakes at no additional cost

TECHNOLOGY COMPLIANCE (16 TAC §84.501):
--------------------------------------------------------------
Our online platform meets all TDLR technology requirements:

  • Timer-Based Progression: Minimum time enforcement per module 
    prevents students from advancing prematurely. Students cannot 
    complete the course in fewer than 6 hours.
  
  • Identity Verification: Photo ID upload and verification at 
    enrollment. Periodic identity confirmation during the course.
  
  • Progress Tracking: Detailed audit logs recording time spent, 
    quiz attempts, login/logout times, and IP addresses.
  
  • Secure Assessment: Questions are randomized from a larger 
    question bank. Each student receives a unique exam sequence.
  
  • Accessibility: WCAG 2.1 compliant. Mobile-responsive design 
    works on smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers.
  
  • Data Security: SSL encryption, encrypted data storage, 
    secure authentication (JWT tokens, bcrypt password hashing).
  
  • Certificate Delivery: Electronic certificate generated 
    upon successful course completion, delivered same day.
  
  • Record Retention: Student records maintained for minimum 
    3 years per TDLR requirements.
