============================================================
Regulatory Body: Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR)
                  + Texas Education Agency (TEA)
Reference: Texas Transportation Code Chapter 1001
           Texas Education Code §1001.101
           16 Texas Administrative Code (TAC) Chapter 84
Timeline: 6-12 weeks for full approval
Estimated Total Cost: ~$1,200-1,500+

STEP 1: PREPARATION
--------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Obtain EIN (see Priority #0 above)
[ ] Register as a Foreign Entity with Texas Secretary of State
    • Website: https://www.sos.state.tx.us
    • Filing: Foreign Sole Proprietorship registration
    • Fee: ~$750 (varies)
    • Designate a Registered Agent in Texas
[ ] Obtain $10,000 Surety Bond
    • Must be from a Texas-admitted surety company
    • Annual premium: typically $100-300 (based on credit)
    • Required per Texas Transportation Code §1001.204
    • Bond must name TDLR as obligee
    • Recommended surety companies:
      - SuretyBonds.com
      - JW Surety Bonds
      - BondExchange.com
    • Note: Get quotes from multiple companies

 CHECKLIST 3: CALIFORNIA — Traffic Violator School Owner
============================================================
Regulatory Body: California DMV, Occupational Licensing Section
Reference: California Vehicle Code §11200-11222
           Title 13 CCR, Division 1, Chapter 1, Article 5
Timeline: 8-16 weeks (CA is typically slower)
Estimated Total Cost: ~$800-1,200+

STEP 1: PREPARATION
--------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Obtain EIN (see Priority #0 above)
[ ] Register with California Secretary of State
    • Foreign business registration
    • Website: https://www.sos.ca.gov
    • Fee: ~$70 for Statement of Information
    • May need Certificate of Qualification for out-of-state business
[ ] Designate a California Registered Agent
    • Required for out-of-state businesses
    • Services like Northwest Registered Agent, LegalZoom, etc.
    • Cost: ~$125-300/year
[ ] Obtain $10,000 Surety Bond
    • Must be from a California-admitted surety company
    • Use Form OL 601 (Surety Bond form from DMV)
    • Annual premium: typically $100-300
    • Required per CVC §11200.5

STEP 2: LIVE SCAN FINGERPRINTING
--------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Complete Live Scan fingerprinting
    • MUST be done at a CA DOJ-authorized Live Scan location
    • You CAN do this from out of state in some cases — 
      contact DMV OL for instructions on out-of-state applicants
    • Alternative: May need to travel to California
    • Cost: ~$50-80 (rolling fee + DOJ/FBI processing)
    • Agency ORI: [Will be on application form]
    • Keep receipt as proof of completion
    • Processing time: 3-7 business days typically
    • NOTE: This is often the longest lead-time item
