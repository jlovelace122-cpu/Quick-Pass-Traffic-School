school offering state-specific, curriculum-compliant driver improvement

and complete audit-trail logging.

Platform highlights:

  • 24/7 availability, fully mobile-responsive

OHIO DIP COURSE

Our Ohio Driver Improvement Program course is a 4-hour (240-minute)
online course specifically aligned with Ohio law and ORC Title 45,
comprehensively covering:

  — Ohio traffic laws (ORC Title 45) and rules of the road
  — OVI laws (ORC § 4511.19) and impaired driving prevention
  — Ohio Move Over law (ORC § 4511.213)
  — Distracted driving and Ohio's texting ban (ORC § 4511.204)
  — Ohio point system (ORC § 4510.036) and insurance consequences
  — Crash causation, dynamics, and prevention techniques
  — Seat belt laws (ORC § 4513.263) and occupant protection
  — School bus safety and school zone regulations
  — Pedestrian and bicycle right-of-way
  — Winter driving and adverse weather conditions
  — Ohio implied consent law (ORC § 4511.191)
