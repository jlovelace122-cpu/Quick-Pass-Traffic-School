CURRICULUM CONTENT:
--------------------------------------------------------------
Our course curriculum covers the following Arizona-specific topics:

Module 1: Introduction & Arizona Traffic Laws (55 minutes)
  - Arizona's point system (8-12 points = TSS requirement)
  - Arizona speed limits and enforcement
  - Hands-Free Law (effective 2021)
  - Move Over Law requirements and penalties
  - Arizona's "Stupid Motorist Law" (ARS 28-910)
  - Seat belt laws and child restraint requirements

Module 2: Defensive Driving Fundamentals (55 minutes)
  - SIPDE defensive driving process
  - Following distance (3-4 second rule)
  - Speed management and stopping distances
  - Intersection safety and red-light running prevention
  - Arizona-specific hazards (wildlife, desert highways, tourist traffic)

Module 3: Impaired and Distracted Driving (55 minutes)
  - Arizona DUI laws (0.08%, Extreme 0.15%, Super Extreme 0.20%)
  - Mandatory jail time penalties (10 days first offense)
  - Implied Consent Law
  - Zero Tolerance for under 21 and drug DUI
  - Hands-free law enforcement and distracted driving penalties
  - Drowsy driving and highway hypnosis

Module 4: Arizona Weather and Road Conditions (55 minutes)
  - Extreme heat preparedness and hot car deaths
  - Monsoon season hazards (June-September)
  - Flash flooding and "Turn Around, Don't Drown"
  - Dust storm safety: "Pull Aside, Stay Alive" protocol
  - Winter driving in northern Arizona
  - Sharing the road (pedestrians, cyclists, trucks)

Assessment:       15-question final exam (multiple choice)
Certificate:      Electronic delivery upon successful completion
