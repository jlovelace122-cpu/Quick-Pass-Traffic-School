ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Quick Pass Traffic School is a professionally developed, technology-forward
online traffic school offering state-specific, curriculum-approved driver
improvement courses. Our platform has been designed from the ground up to
meet the rigorous requirements of state DMV licensing programs and to
provide an effective, accessible driver education experience.

Our platform features:
  • Custom-built, time-gated course delivery ensuring minimum instruction hours
  • Identity verification via government-issued photo ID
  • Randomized question banks with 500+ exam questions to prevent cheating
  • Real-time session logging, anti-tab-switch detection, and audit trails
  • Same-day electronic certificate delivery upon successful completion
  • Automated state reporting infrastructure (currently live for Florida)
  • 24/7 student support and fully responsive mobile-compatible interface

GEORGIA COURSE

Our Georgia Defensive Driving Course is a 6-hour (360-minute) online course
specifically tailored to Georgia law and O.C.G.A. Title 40, covering:

  — Georgia traffic laws and regulations
  — Crash causation, dynamics, and prevention
  — DUI and impaired driving (O.C.G.A. § 40-6-391)
  — Distracted driving (O.C.G.A. § 40-6-241 — hands-free law)
  — Move Over Law (O.C.G.A. § 40-6-16)
  — Aggressive driving and road rage
  — Pedestrian, cyclist, and vulnerable road user safety
  — Seat belt and occupant protection laws
  — Georgia point system and insurance implications
