============================================================

You need TWO $10,000 surety bonds:
  1. Texas — TDLR Driving Safety Course Provider Bond
  2. California — DMV Traffic Violator School Owner Bond (OL 601)

You can often get both from the same surety company.
Apply to 2-3 companies and compare premiums.

ESTIMATED COST:

  - With good credit, you may pay as little as $100 each

============================================================
HOW TO APPLY — STEP BY STEP
============================================================

1. Go to one of the recommended websites above
   (SuretyBonds.com is usually the fastest)

2. Search for:
   - Texas: "Texas Driving Safety Course Provider Bond"
   - California: "California Traffic Violator School Bond" 
     or "DMV OL 601 Bond"

3. Fill in the application using the information above

4. You'll get an instant or same-day quote showing your 
   annual premium

5. Pay the premium (credit card usually accepted)

6. The surety company will:
   - Issue the bond document
   - Use the correct state-specific form (OL 601 for CA)
   - Mail or email you the executed bond
   - Some can send the original directly to the state agency

7. Include the ORIGINAL executed bond with your state 
   application package

8. Keep copies for your records

9. Set a calendar reminder to renew before expiration


============================================================
IMPORTANT NOTES
============================================================

• You do NOT need the EIN to get the bonds — you can use 
  your SSN initially and update the EIN later

• Apply for bonds NOW — don't wait for EIN. The bonds take 
  1-3 business days to issue.

• If your credit is 650+, expect premiums around $100-150 
  per bond per year

• If your credit is below 650, premiums may be higher 
  ($200-400) or you may need to provide collateral

• Arizona does NOT require a surety bond (just TX and CA)

• Keep both bonds active for as long as you hold the state 
  licenses — lapse = license suspension
