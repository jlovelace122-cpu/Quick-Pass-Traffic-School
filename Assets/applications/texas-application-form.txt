C. CURRICULUM OUTLINE (6 Hours, 6 Modules)
--------------------------------------------------------------
Per 16 TAC §84.400 — Course Content Requirements

Module 1: Texas Traffic Laws & Driver Responsibilities
(60 minutes)
  - Texas Transportation Code fundamentals
  - Speed limits (including 85 mph on SH 130)
  - Right-of-way laws
  - Traffic control devices and signals
  - Driver licensing requirements
  - Financial responsibility (insurance)
  - Move Over/Slow Down Law (TRC §545.157)
  - Hands-Free Law (effective 2017)
  - Consequences of traffic violations
  - Texas point system overview

Module 2: Defensive Driving Principles
(60 minutes)
  - IPDE process (Identify, Predict, Decide, Execute)
  - Smith System of driving
  - 3-second following distance
  - Space cushion management
  - Mirror usage and blind spot coverage
  - Intersection navigation
  - Highway/freeway driving
  - Lane change safety
  - Speed management and stopping distance

Module 3: Impaired Driving — Texas DWI Laws
(60 minutes)
  - Texas DWI laws (NOT "DUI" — Texas uses "DWI")
  - Texas Penal Code §49.04-49.09
  - BAC limits: 0.08% (standard), 0.04% (commercial),
    0.00% (under 21)
  - Zero Tolerance for minors
  - Implied Consent (TRC §724)
  - DWI penalties (1st, 2nd, 3rd+ offense)
  - Intoxication Assault and Intoxication Manslaughter
  - Drug-impaired driving
  - Open container laws (TRC §49.031)
  - ALR (Administrative License Revocation) process
  - Surcharge/Driver Responsibility Program (historical context)

Module 4: Distracted Driving in Texas
(60 minutes)
  - Texas Hands-Free Law (TRC §545.4251)
  - Three types of distraction (visual, manual, cognitive)
  - Cell phone ban in school zones
  - Under-18 complete wireless ban
  - Texting while driving prohibition
  - Consequences of distracted driving crashes
  - Technology and driving
  - Work zone distraction dangers

Module 5: Texas Road Hazards & Weather
(60 minutes)
  - "Turn Around, Don't Drown" (flash flooding)
  - Hurricane evacuation driving
  - Ice storm and black ice (North TX/Panhandle)
  - Dust storms (West Texas)
  - Construction zone safety ("Fines Double" zones)
  - Night driving techniques
  - Rural highway driving considerations
  - Wildlife crossings (deer, livestock)
  - Railroad crossing safety

Module 6: Sharing the Road & Course Review
(60 minutes)
  - Pedestrian safety and right-of-way
  - Bicycle safety (3-foot passing law)
  - Motorcycle awareness
  - Large truck "No-Zones"
  - School bus laws (TRC §545.066)
  - Emergency vehicle response
  - Road rage and aggressive driving prevention
  - Fatigue and drowsy driving
  - Comprehensive course review
  - Final exam preparation

--------------------------------------------------------------
D. ASSESSMENT STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------

Module Quizzes:
  - One quiz per module (6 total)
  - 4 questions each (24 total quiz questions)
  - 70% passing score required
  - Must pass quiz to advance to next module

Final Examination:
  - 20 questions, multiple choice
  - 80% passing score required (16/20 correct)
  - Questions randomly selected from 50+ question bank
  - Answer options randomized per attempt
  - Each student receives unique exam configuration
  - Unlimited retakes at no additional charge
