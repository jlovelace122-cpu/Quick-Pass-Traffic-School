CURRICULUM CONTENT (Per Title 13 CCR §345.56):
--------------------------------------------------------------
Our 8-hour curriculum has been developed in compliance with DMV 
curriculum requirements and covers all mandated topics:

Module 1: Introduction & California Vehicle Code (70 minutes)
  - California traffic law fundamentals
  - NOTS point system (4 pts/12 mo, 6 pts/24 mo, 8 pts/36 mo)
  - Traffic Violator School eligibility requirements
  - Speed laws (Basic Speed Law, Maximum Speed Law, 65/70 mph)
  - Right-of-way rules
  - Hands-Free Law (CVC §23123, §23123.5)
  - Move Over/Slow Down Law (CVC §21809)
  - Financial responsibility requirements

Module 2: Defensive Driving Techniques (70 minutes)
  - 3-second following distance rule
  - SIPDE scanning process
  - Space cushion management
  - Mirror usage and blind spot elimination
  - Intersection navigation and left-turn hazards
  - Freeway driving (merging, lane changes, California highways)
  - Speed management and stopping distance physics

Module 3: Impaired Driving & California DUI Laws (70 minutes)
  - California DUI laws (CVC §23152, §23153)
  - BAC limits: 0.08% (standard), 0.04% (commercial), 
    0.01% (under 21)
  - Implied Consent Law (CVC §23612)
  - Administrative Per Se (APS) process
  - DUI penalties and enhancements
  - Drug-impaired driving (including marijuana — legal but 
    not while driving)
  - Zero Tolerance for under-21 drivers

Module 4: Distracted Driving (60 minutes)
  - California Hands-Free Law enforcement
  - Three types of distraction
  - CVC §23123 (handheld phone prohibition)
  - CVC §23123.5 (texting prohibition)
  - Under-18 complete wireless device ban
  - Work zone and school zone considerations
  - Cognitive distraction research

Module 5: Sharing California Roads (60 minutes)
  - "Three Feet for Safety Act" (CVC §21760) — cyclists
  - Lane splitting: legal in California (AB 51)
  - Motorcycle awareness
  - Pedestrian right-of-way rules
  - Large truck "No-Zones" (blind spots)
  - School bus laws
  - Emergency vehicle response (CVC §21806)
  - Railroad crossings

Module 6: California Weather & Road Conditions (60 minutes)
  - Rain driving and hydroplaning
  - Tule fog (Central Valley visibility hazard)
  - Mountain driving (grades, curves, chain requirements)
  - Chain installation requirements (R1, R2, R3 levels)
  - Earthquake safety while driving
  - Wildfire smoke and air quality driving
  - Night driving techniques
  - Construction zone safety

Module 7: Fatigue, Emotions & Course Review (60 minutes)
  - Drowsy driving recognition and prevention
  - Road rage and aggressive driving
  - Emotional fitness to drive
  - Stress management techniques
  - Comprehensive course review
  - Final exam preparation strategies
  - California-specific law review summary

Assessment Structure:
  - 4 quiz questions per module (28 total, 70% passing)
  - 20-question final exam (multiple choice, 80% passing)
  - Questions drawn from 50+ question bank, randomized
  - Unlimited retakes at no additional charge
