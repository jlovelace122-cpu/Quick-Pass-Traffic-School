SECTION 6: CURRICULUM SUMMARY (8 Hours, 7 Modules)
--------------------------------------------------------------
Per Title 13 CCR §345.56

Module 1: Introduction & California Vehicle Code
(70 minutes)
  - California traffic law fundamentals
  - NOTS point system (4/12, 6/24, 8/36 thresholds)
  - TVS eligibility requirements
  - Speed laws (Basic Speed Law, Max Speed Law)
  - Right-of-way rules
  - Hands-Free Law (CVC §23123, §23123.5)
  - Move Over/Slow Down Law (CVC §21809)
  - Financial responsibility requirements

Module 2: Defensive Driving Techniques
(70 minutes)
  - 3-second following distance
  - SIPDE scanning process
  - Space cushion management
  - Mirror usage and blind spots
  - Intersection navigation
  - Freeway driving (California highways)
  - Speed management and stopping distance

Module 3: Impaired Driving & California DUI Laws
(70 minutes)
  - CVC §23152, §23153 (DUI/DUI with injury)
  - BAC limits: 0.08% / 0.04% / 0.01% (under 21)
  - Implied Consent Law (CVC §23612)
  - Administrative Per Se (APS) process
  - DUI penalties and enhancements
  - Drug-impaired driving (incl. marijuana)
  - Zero Tolerance for under-21

Module 4: Distracted Driving
(60 minutes)
  - California Hands-Free Law enforcement
  - CVC §23123 (handheld phone)
  - CVC §23123.5 (texting)
  - Under-18 complete wireless ban
  - Three types of distraction
  - Work zone and school zone focus

Module 5: Sharing California Roads
(60 minutes)
  - Three Feet for Safety Act (CVC §21760)
  - Lane splitting: legal in CA (AB 51)
  - Motorcycle awareness
  - Pedestrian right-of-way
  - Large truck "No-Zones"
  - School bus laws
  - Emergency vehicle response (CVC §21806)
  - Railroad crossings

Module 6: California Weather & Road Conditions
(60 minutes)
  - Rain driving and hydroplaning
  - Tule fog (Central Valley hazard)
  - Mountain driving (chains: R1/R2/R3)
  - Earthquake safety while driving
  - Wildfire smoke driving precautions
  - Night driving techniques
  - Construction zone safety

Module 7: Fatigue, Emotions & Course Review
(60 minutes)
  - Drowsy driving recognition
  - Road rage and aggressive driving
  - Emotional fitness to drive
  - Comprehensive course review
  - Final exam preparation
