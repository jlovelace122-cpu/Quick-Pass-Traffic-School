INDIANA COURSE

Our Indiana Driving Safety Course is designed to meet Indiana's IC 9-30-9
requirements and covers all topics mandated for DSC providers, including:

  — Indiana traffic laws (IC Title 9) and rules of the road
  — OWI/OVWI laws and impaired driving prevention (IC 9-30-5)
  — Indiana Move Over law (IC 9-21-8-35)
  — Distracted driving and Indiana's hands-free law (HEA 1236)
  — Crash causation, dynamics, and prevention
  — Agricultural vehicle and rural road safety
  — School bus safety, school zones, and pedestrian protection
  — Indiana point system and insurance implications
  — Railroad grade crossing safety

The course is delivered fully online, is self-paced with time enforcement,
and is accessible 24/7 from any device.
