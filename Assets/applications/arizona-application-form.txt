SECTION C: CURRICULUM OUTLINE
============================================================

Module 1: Arizona Traffic Laws & Driving Responsibilities
(60 minutes)
  - Arizona Vehicle Code fundamentals
  - Speed limits and speed law violations
  - Right-of-way rules
  - Traffic signals and signage
  - Points system overview
  - Financial responsibility (insurance requirements)
  - Hands-Free Law
  - Move Over Law (ARS §28-775)
  - Consequences of traffic violations

Module 2: Defensive Driving & Hazard Recognition
(60 minutes)
  - SIPDE scanning method
  - 3-second following distance rule
  - Space cushion management
  - Mirror usage and blind spots
  - Intersection safety
  - Freeway driving techniques
  - Speed management and braking distance
  - Night driving considerations

Module 3: Impaired & Distracted Driving
(60 minutes)
  - Arizona DUI laws (ARS §28-1381 through §28-1383)
  - BAC limits: 0.08% standard, 0.04% commercial, 
    0.00% under 21
  - Three-tier DUI enforcement:
    • Regular DUI (0.08%+) — 10 days jail minimum
    • Extreme DUI (0.15%+) — 30 days jail minimum
    • Super Extreme DUI (0.20%+) — 45 days jail minimum
  - Implied Consent Law
  - Drug-impaired driving
  - Three types of distraction
  - Cell phone and texting laws
  - Hands-Free Law compliance

Module 4: Arizona-Specific Hazards & Sharing the Road
(60 minutes)
  - Stupid Motorist Law (ARS §28-910) — flooded roadways
  - Pull Aside Stay Alive — dust storms/haboobs
  - Extreme heat driving precautions
  - Desert highway driving
  - Wildlife crossings
  - Sharing roads with pedestrians and cyclists
  - School zone safety
  - Emergency vehicle response
  - Construction zone safety
  - Road rage and aggressive driving prevention
  - Course review and exam preparation
